Aqua Essence

Worked with a real client for four months to build a system that matches swim instructors with students for a program with around 1,800 swimmers a week.

Main Features

  • Working With The Client: Worked with my team and the client to understand what the schedule needed and made changes based on their feedback.
  • Matching Students: Updated the system to keep existing classes and instructor relationships, then used OR-Tools CP-SAT to match the students who were left.
  • Testing The Algorithms: Built a tool that tests the matching algorithms with larger datasets and compares their speed and results.
