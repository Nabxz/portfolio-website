PhysiciansConnect

PhysiciansConnect is a healthcare coordination platform for physicians and receptionists. It streamlines appointment scheduling, patient history access, prescriptions, referrals, billing & invoicing, and internal messaging. Built in Java with a 3-tier architecture (UI, services, persistence) on an embedded SQLite database to ensure modularity, scalability, and ease of testing. Desktop UI powered by Swing, with comprehensive JUnit 5 coverage and production-ready Gradle configuration.

Key Features And Workflows

  • Appointment Slot Management: Create, update, and remove physician availability; handle booking conflicts and notifications.
  • Patient Records & History: View visit summaries, exam details, and longitudinal timelines for informed decision-making.
  • Prescriptions: Search formulary, add dosage and notes, and record prescription history per patient.
  • Billing & Insurance: Generate invoices, track payments, and apply insurance adjustments with auditability.
  • Referrals & Messaging: Create/track referrals to specialists and use an internal messaging system for team coordination.
  • Notifications: Real-time UI alerts for appointment updates, new messages, and billing events.
  • Profile & Photos: Local profile photo storage for physicians/receptionists with automatic resizing (200×200).
  • Architecture: Clear separation of concerns—Presentation (Swing), Business Logic (managers/services), Persistence (SQLite JDBC). Swap persistence implementations without touching UI/logic.
